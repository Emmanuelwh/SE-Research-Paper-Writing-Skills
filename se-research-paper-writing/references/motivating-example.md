# Motivating Example and Preliminary Study Guide

## Goal

Use this section to make the problem concrete before Methodology. A Motivating Example should show why the task is hard and why existing methods fail. A Preliminary Study should provide early empirical evidence that motivates RQs, taxonomy design, or method requirements.

## Motivating Example Structure

1. Introduce the concrete case, artifact, or workflow.
2. Show what a human or current tool observes.
3. Explain why naive/current reasoning fails.
4. Identify the hidden difficulty, missing evidence, or non-obvious relation.
5. Connect the example to the challenges in Introduction and the design in Methodology.

## Preliminary Study Structure

1. State the purpose of the preliminary study.
2. Describe the sampled data, artifacts, participants, or cases.
3. Explain the analysis protocol briefly.
4. Report only motivation-level findings, not full final results.
5. Derive implications for RQs, taxonomy, method design, or evaluation setup.

## Writing Rules

1. Keep the example small enough for reviewers to follow.
2. Do not overload the section with final evaluation numbers.
3. Use figures, tables, or step-by-step traces when they reduce cognitive load.
4. Make the failure of current practice explicit: `[what it sees] -> [wrong/missing interpretation] -> [consequence]`.
5. End with a clear bridge to Methodology: `This example motivates the need for [capability/challenge].`

## Semi-Template

`Consider [case/artifact/workflow]. Existing practice/tool [how it works] and therefore observes [surface evidence]. However, [hidden mechanism/context] changes the interpretation: [why the surface evidence is insufficient]. As a result, [limitation/consequence]. This example shows that a solution must [capability 1], [capability 2], and [capability 3].`

## Checklist

1. Does the example directly support the paper's problem?
2. Does it show why current solutions fail?
3. Does it motivate the challenges in Introduction?
4. Does it prepare the Methodology without duplicating it?
5. Is the example anonymized or ethically safe when needed?
